Eve Machine

A free desktop companion for EVE Online. Log in your characters once and see where your stuff is, what it's worth, what to build, what you mined and who just jumped into local.

Download for Windows All releases
Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · Free

Everything about your characters, in one place

Every tab links to the others: click an item to see its market, where you own it, or how to build it.

Overview

What needs your attention today across all your characters.

Assets

Everything your characters own (and your corporation's, with the Director role), searchable by item, place and owner, with market values.

Market

Prices, price history, trade hub comparison, and how your open orders stand against the competition.

Industry

Blueprint planner, material trees, stockpiles, build goals and production pipelines.

Mining

Your mining ledger, plus live m³ per hour, ISK per hour and ore hold alerts read from your game logs.

Planetary

Colony status, extraction timers, production planning and what your planets earn.

Local Intel

Paste local chat to see each pilot's threat level, known gankers and recent kills nearby.

Combat & Missions

Damage, hits and bounties from your combat logs, LP and ISK earnings, and cheat sheets for sites and missions.

Hauling

Haul tasks and a route map.

Overlays

Small always-on-top windows for mining and combat while you play.

And the rest

Wallet, skills, todos, appraisals, a program launcher and backups of your EVE client settings.

Stays up to date

Tells you when a new version is out, shows what changed, and installs it when you choose.

Install

Takes about a minute.

  1. Download EveMachine-Setup.exe with the button above.
  2. Run it. It installs for your Windows user only (no administrator prompt) and adds Start Menu and desktop shortcuts.
  3. Add a character. Click Add Character and log in on EVE Online's own website in your normal browser. Eve Machine never sees your password.
  4. Get the game data. On first launch the app offers to download CCP's latest static data (items, blueprints, map). It takes a minute.
"Windows protected your PC"? The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Privacy

There is no Eve Machine server, account or analytics.

  • Everything stays on your computer: login tokens (encrypted with your Windows account), synced character data, settings and notes.
  • You log in on CCP's website, so Eve Machine never sees your EVE password. You can revoke its access any time at community.eveonline.com.
  • Game and chat logs are read on your computer for the features that use them, and never uploaded.
  • The app talks directly to CCP's ESI and login servers, EVE Tycoon (prices), zKillboard (public kill stats in Local Intel), the EVE University wiki, Fuzzwork, Janice (only with your own API key) and GitHub (update checks).
